## Description (4,000)

Safience is a web browser for people who work in web apps: a design tool, a whiteboard, a project board, a CMS. Pages load in their desktop versions, your trackpad and keyboard reach them the way they do on a computer, and your spaces, bookmarks and tabs follow you from iPad to iPhone, and to Chrome at your desk.

MADE FOR THE TRACKPAD AND KEYBOARD
• Pinch to zoom and two-finger scrolling go to the page, so a design canvas zooms where you point.
• ⌘ shortcuts belong to the page: undo, find, copy, and every web app's own. Safience keeps only the keys every browser keeps, ⌘T, ⌘W and ⌘N; the rest of its shortcuts use Control and Option, out of the way.
• Pages' own cursors show, so a design tool's pen, frame and comment cursors look as they should.

ON IPHONE TOO
• The bar sits at the bottom, under your thumb. Swipe up for your tabs as cards, with your pinned tabs in a dock.
• Desktop View lays out the full-size page and turns the screen into a trackpad: one finger moves the cursor, a tap clicks, two fingers scroll. Hover styles and cursors show, and a minimap shows where you are on the page.
• On an iPhone Duo, folded you get the phone layout; unfolded, the desktop version.

TABS THAT STAY OUT OF THE WAY
• One row for your tabs and the address. Click the tab you're on to type a new address.
• Split view puts two tabs side by side in one window. Drag the divider for more room.
• Pinned tabs keep the apps you use every day as icons at the front.
• The bars take on each page's colour, so page and browser read as one.

A SPACE FOR EVERY CLIENT
• Each space signs in on its own, so two accounts of the same web app stay signed in at once.
• Give each space a colour and an icon; its button tells you which space you're in.
• Every space keeps its own bookmarks, in folders, shown as a grid on a new tab.
• Bring bookmarks over from Chrome, Firefox or Safari.

EVERYWHERE YOU WORK
• Turn on iCloud, and your spaces, their bookmarks and pinned tabs show on every iPad and iPhone signed in to your account. It goes through your own iCloud, with no server in between.
• Each device's open tabs show on the others, so you pick up where you left off.
• Safience Sync for Chrome pairs a Chrome profile with a space: its bookmarks bar and pinned tabs stay in sync, and its open tabs show in Safience. Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers.
• Sign-ins and site data stay on each device.

YOUR DEFAULT BROWSER
• Make Safience the default browser on iPad and iPhone, and links from other apps open in it.
• Passkeys work on every site, and Share Page offers Add to Home Screen.

FAST FROM THE KEYBOARD
• The command palette (Control-Option-K) finds tabs in every space, spaces, bookmarks and commands.
• Open several windows side by side with Stage Manager.

LIGHT ON YOUR DEVICE
• Heavy tabs you're not using sleep to save memory, and wake when you open them.
• Safience collects no data and doesn't track you.
